# Frequency Match — access

Collide is **free**. Frequency Pro ($4.99/mo via Whop) is the optional deep toolkit.

| Stage | What happens |
|-------|----------------|
| Land on site | Collide immediately. No membership wall. |
| Join free | Optional. `https://thefirstspark.shop/join.html` (email gate, source `join-free`) |
| Sign in | Magic link. Needed for Pro unlock, not for a first collide. |
| Optional | Frequency Pro $4.99/mo — saved history, deeper analysis, relationship lens |

`ACCESS_MODE` in `js/config.js`:

- `open` (live) — anyone can collide
- `members` — Pro or `is_player` before collide

Free Sparkverse membership is the email door on thefirstspark.shop, not a Whop free Lobby.
